Business Manager by RootRecord

A practical workspace for managing the work behind a business. Keep the moving parts of your work in one place.

What it does

Business Manager brings time, clients, money, inventory, and the schedule into one Android app. Sign-in and synced features need an internet connection. It is a Root Record product, listed on Google Play as Business Manager by RootRecord.

Key capabilities

  • Time tracking
  • Clients
  • Income
  • Expenses
  • Categories
  • Inventory
  • Scheduling
  • Recurring work
  • Work logs
  • Reports
  • Exports

Who it is for

People who want the work behind a business in one place: time, clients, money, stock, and the schedule, without splitting that work across a stack of separate tools.
