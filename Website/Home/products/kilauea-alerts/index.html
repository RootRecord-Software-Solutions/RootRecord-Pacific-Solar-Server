Kilauea Alerts

Focused information and alerts for Kīlauea activity and local conditions.

Kilauea Alerts is an independent Root Record app. It is not produced or endorsed by the USGS, NOAA, the National Weather Service, or any government agency. Information is compiled from public official feeds. In an emergency, follow Hawaiʻi County Civil Defense and official instructions.

What it does

The app brings Kīlauea-related status, recent earthquake information, local weather, and alerts into one focused presentation. It is built to reduce noise around the readings that matter for the volcano and the island around it.

Key capabilities

  • Kīlauea activity
  • Earthquake information
  • Weather information
  • Alerts
  • Local conditions
  • Focused presentation

Who it is for

People who live on Hawaiʻi Island, visit, or follow Kīlauea from somewhere else, and want those readings in one place.
