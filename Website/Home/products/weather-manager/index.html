Weather Manager

Focused weather information without unnecessary complexity. Get the weather information that matters, when it matters.

What it does

Weather Manager is a Root Record application for current conditions and the outlook ahead. It is built to stay on the information you came for: temperature, how it feels, wind, humidity, the hours ahead, a longer outlook, and alerts.

Key capabilities

  • Current conditions
  • Temperature
  • Feels-like temperature
  • Wind
  • Humidity
  • Hourly outlook
  • Extended outlook
  • Weather alerts
  • Location-based forecasts
  • Saved locations where supported

Who it is for

People who want a clear reading of conditions and the outlook, including saved locations where the app supports them, without extra layers in the way.
